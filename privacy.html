Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how NexusFrame, a desktop video creation and publishing tool, handles information when you use its features or connect third-party accounts.

1. Information involved

Depending on the features you choose to use, NexusFrame may process videos, captions, publishing schedules, local application settings, and authorization credentials for connected services. When you authorize a TikTok connection, the integration may request basic account information and permissions to upload or publish videos, as shown on TikTok's authorization screen. Access is limited by the permissions you grant and the integration's approval status.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to prepare content, authenticate authorized connections, send publishing requests, display status, troubleshoot errors, and support the workflows you initiate. NexusFrame does not sell personal information or use connected account information for targeted advertising.

3. Storage and sharing

NexusFrame is designed to run on the user's computer. Local project files, settings, and credentials may be stored on that device. When you request publication, the necessary content and authorization information are transmitted to the selected platform or its API. Third-party services handle information under their own policies. Users should protect access to their computer and local credential files.

4. Retention and deletion

Local files and credentials remain on the user's device until removed by the user or by application functionality where available. You can stop using NexusFrame, remove its locally stored files and credentials, and revoke third-party access in the connected service's security settings. For help identifying or deleting NexusFrame-related data, email the contact below. Deleting local data does not automatically remove content already published to third-party platforms.

5. Security

We aim to limit access to the permissions needed for the selected functionality. No software or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Do not share access tokens or account credentials publicly.

6. Your choices

You control which accounts you connect and which content you choose to publish. You may revoke permissions through the third-party platform and request assistance with privacy-related questions or data deletion.

7. Children

NexusFrame is not directed to children under 13. Users must satisfy the minimum age and other requirements of connected platforms.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as NexusFrame's features evolve. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
